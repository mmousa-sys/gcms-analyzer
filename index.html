Designed for access

Use it online. Take it offline.

Start in the browser, then keep a self-contained copy of the suite on your computer or USB drive for offline use.

The downloadable edition includes the applications and charting library in one HTML file. The suite itself does not need an internet connection once downloaded; publication links do.

03

Double-click Lab_Technician_Suite_Offline.html and choose a recent browser if asked.

04

No installer is needed. Most tools work offline; the 3D viewer and external links need internet.
